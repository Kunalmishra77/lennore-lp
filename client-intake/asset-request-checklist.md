## Important: health claims
Ads for alkaline water machines are disapproved by Google/Meta if the page claims health outcomes, and claims about diseases like diabetes are restricted by Indian law. The new page will focus on how the machine works, adjustable levels, filtration (with test report), design, installation, service and warranty. Please don't send testimonials about health improvements — taste, service, design and ease-of-use stories are perfect.
